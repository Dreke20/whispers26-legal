Privacy Policy

Whispers & Warnings ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy, personal safety, and confidentiality of both our clients ("Seekers") and our spiritual practitioners ("Readers"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and delete information when you use the Whispers & Warnings Android application and our companion web portal at whispers26.com.

Strict Zero-PII Peer Privacy Guarantee:

Clients and Readers never see each other's phone numbers, email addresses, last names, or physical addresses. All audio, video, and text readings run through private encrypted in-app channels. Readers receive only a sanitized first name or screen alias.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Details: Email address (for authentication only), chosen screen name or stage name, optional zodiac sign, spiritual focus/specialties, and role (Client or Reader).
  • Session & Communication Data: Text chat messages sent during live sessions, reading duration timestamps, and client/reader safety reports. Audio and video streams are transmitted via encrypted peer-to-peer WebRTC channels and are never recorded.
  • Live Reading Balance & In-App Purchases: In-app credit top-ups are processed directly and securely through Google Play In-App Billing (Google LLC). Whispers & Warnings never receives, handles, or stores your credit card numbers or payment details. The app records only your prepaid live reading balance and transaction ledger.
  • Device Permissions: Microphone (voice/video readings), Camera (optional video readings), and Notifications (incoming call alerts and reader online alerts).

2. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate accounts, route live 1:1 human reading requests, deliver spiritual guidance services, and enforce per-minute billing and automatic connection-drop refunds.
  • To enforce Trust & Safety through user blocking, moderation reporting, and fraud prevention.

3. Data Storage & Infrastructure Providers

We do not sell your personal information. Platform data is hosted and processed strictly with trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Google Play Billing: Handles all in-app purchase transactions securely through Google Play Services under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Fly.io: Backend server and real-time WebRTC signaling runtime. All application data in transit and at rest is securely stored and processed on Fly.io servers.
  • Supabase: Encrypted authentication and PostgreSQL database storage for account profiles and reading balance records.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used solely and exclusively to deliver push notifications (e.g. incoming live reading call alerts and reader availability alerts). FCM is not used for any other purpose.

4. No Analytics, No Advertising IDs, and No Ads

Whispers & Warnings is strictly private and non-commercialized in its data practices:

  • The app does not use analytics of any kind (Firebase Analytics and measurement libraries are completely removed).
  • The app does not collect, read, or transmit Advertising IDs (all Google Advertising ID / AD_ID and Android AdServices permissions are explicitly stripped).
  • The app does not show advertisements and does not sell or share user data with advertising networks or data brokers.

5. User Safety: Reporting & Blocking

Users and readers can report inappropriate behavior or block another participant directly from any active chat/session screen or reader profile. Blocking immediately terminates active communication and prevents future contact.

6. Account Deletion & Data Retention

You may delete your account and personal data at any time:

  • In the App: Go to Account → Delete Account & Data (Clients) or Settings → Delete Reader Account & Data (Readers).
  • On the Web: Visit our Web Account Deletion Portal.

Upon deletion, your profile, credentials, journal entries, blocked lists, and personal chat history are permanently purged within 30 days. Minimal anonymized ledger records may be retained strictly where required for financial and tax compliance.

7. Age Restriction (18+)

Whispers & Warnings is strictly for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors under 18.

8. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries or manual deletion assistance, email us at support@whispers26.com or visit https://www.whispers26.com.
