Request Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your Whispers & Warnings account and associated personal data either directly inside the Android app or by submitting this web deletion form.

Option 1: Web Account Deletion Request

Enter the email address associated with your Client or Reader account. Your request will be processed automatically or queued for verified deletion within 72 hours (completed in full within 30 days).

Option 2: Instant In-App Deletion

  • Clients / Seekers: Open the app → tap the Account tab → scroll down and tap Delete Account & Data → confirm deletion.
  • Readers / Advisors: Open the app → tap the Settings tab → scroll to Account Control and tap Delete Reader Account & Data → confirm deletion.

Data Deleted: Profile details, authentication tokens, spiritual focus, saved tarot journals, reader notes, and chat history.
Retained Only Where Required by Law: Anonymized tax/financial ledger records (non-identifying) for statutory audit compliance.
