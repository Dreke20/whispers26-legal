Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026
Application: ShrinkPro – Photo & PDF Shrink (com.shrinkpro.toolkit)

ShrinkPro ("we", "our", or "us") is an on-device media optimization app. Your photos, documents, PDFs, videos, and audio are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to ShrinkPro servers.

1. Media and file access (on-device processing)

  • No cloud uploads of your media. Photo compression, image resizing, format conversion (JPEG, PNG, WebP), PDF generation, and video or audio processing happen on your device.
  • Photos and files you select. On Android, ShrinkPro uses the system photo or file picker (and Android share-sheet intents) so it only accesses the specific items you choose. It does not request broad background access to your photo library (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES or READ_MEDIA_VIDEO).
  • Camera. When you choose to take a new photo in the app, ShrinkPro requests the Camera permission and uses the device camera only for that capture. The photo is then processed on your device and is not uploaded to our servers. ShrinkPro does not use the microphone and does not request microphone permission.
  • Temporary cache. Processed output files are kept in the app’s private cache so you can save or share them, and are cleared by the operating system or when you clear app data.

2. Information stored locally on your device

ShrinkPro stores lightweight preferences and usage history on your device (for example with on-device key-value storage), including:

  • File optimization summaries (file sizes before and after, and timestamps).
  • Active membership (Pro) status, and any temporary Pro pass granted after you watch a rewarded ad. That pass lasts 5 minutes per rewarded ad, not 24 hours.

You can erase this local history inside ShrinkPro → Pro & Settings → Clear Local Optimization History, or by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage in Android Settings.

3. Third-party services (advertising and in-app purchases)

If you use the free, ad-supported version or purchase ShrinkPro Pro, third-party services approved for Google Play may process limited device or purchase information:

  1. Google AdMob (advertising). Used in the free tier to show banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video ads. AdMob may use your device’s Advertising ID, coarse IP-based location, and ad-interaction diagnostics to serve non-personalized or personalized ads (subject to your consent in applicable regions such as the EEA and UK). Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  2. Google Play Billing and RevenueCat (purchases and subscriptions). Payments, renewals, and refunds are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not see or store your credit card or payment details. RevenueCat processes purchase receipts and entitlement status so your Pro unlock can be restored. RevenueCat’s privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. Managing subscriptions and your data

  • Cancel subscriptions: Google Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or use the subscriptions link in the app.
  • Reset Advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording may vary by device).
  • Delete local data: use Clear Local Optimization History in the app, or clear app storage / uninstall.

5. Children’s privacy

ShrinkPro is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
